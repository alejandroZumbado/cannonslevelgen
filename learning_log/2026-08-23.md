Reasoning given: The new policy reasons about how much damage a cannon can actually deliver before the leading pirate in its column reaches the loss position. It computes the *deficit* – the extra damage points that must be added (by spawning/merging) so the cannon can kill the front pirate in time. The action chooser first fixes any column that still has a deficit (by spawning into that column, which merges and raises its damage). If no deficits exist it places new cannons in empty but threatened columns, and only when every column is already safe does it move the weakest cannon onto the most dangerous column to give it an extra damage point. This deterministic, damage‑budget approach removes many unnecessary spawns and ensures merges are performed exactly when required, raising the win‑rate.
