The champion policy rewards “reserved” cannons – cannons that sit in empty columns – with a `‑reserved` term in the decision key.  When a column contains a queue of pirates, this incentive can cause the algorithm to move a cannon away (or to waste the pending spawn) instead of merging to raise damage, which is exactly what lets it lose the stacked‑column levels (e.g., level 38, column 1).
