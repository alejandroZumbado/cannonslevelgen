## strategy_learner: PROMOTED

win rate 87.50% -> 89.42% (avg rounds 7.0 -> 7.0)

Reasoning given: **Change summary**  
When two actions are otherwise equal (same deficit, same current max pirate position and same optimistic two‑round outlook) the original policy breaks ties by the raw action tuple, which makes *move* actions win over *spawn* actions because `"move"` < `"spawn"` lexicographically. In tight situations a move discards the pending spawn cannon that could be needed next round, causing unnecessary losses. The fix adds an explicit tie‑breaker that prefers *spawn* actions over *move* actions whenever the evaluated scores are identical.
