## strategy_learner: rejected (no improvement)

candidate win rate 93.37% vs current 93.52%

Reasoning given: **Change summary** – The current `deficit` treats a whole column as if the cannon can hit *all* pirates there each round (it uses `hp_sum – dmg*steps`). When several pirates are stacked, only the front pirate can be hit each round, so the true damage that can be dealt before the front reaches the loss position is often far less than `dmg*steps`. This makes the policy underestimate the “deficit” for stacked columns and it fails to merge enough damage early (as seen in Levels 179‑180).  
**Fix** – Replace the simple `deficit` with a precise simulation that, for each column, applies the cannon’s damage round‑by‑round to the front pirate, removes dead pirates, and continues for the remaining steps. The remaining HP after those steps is the true deficit. All other logic stays identical.

Reasoning given: **Change summary** – The original `deficit` only counted the rounds until the *front* pirate would reach position 3 (`steps = MAX_POSITION‑front.position`). I changed `deficit` to add `len(col_pirates)-1` to the step count, giving a correct estimate of damage that can be dealt before any pirate in that column would cause a loss. This prevents the policy from over‑prioritising stacked columns and lets it merge or move cannons more sensibly.
